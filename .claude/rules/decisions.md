# Decisions

- `docs/decisions.md` holds only decisions in force: `## D-NNN Title (YYYY-MM-DD)`, then Context, Decision and Consequences in three to six lines. A decision that no longer holds is removed with the change that ends it.
- Nothing private goes into the file: no personal matters, credentials, hardware or schedules.
- Plans and ideas are GitHub issues, not entries here; `CLAUDE.md` says how they are labelled.
- `sh scripts/check-docs.sh` checks the wording.
